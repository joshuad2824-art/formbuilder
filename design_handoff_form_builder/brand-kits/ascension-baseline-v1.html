The green correction, measured. White on #00a791 is 3.03:1 — it clears the 3:1 large-text floor by one hundredth and fails body size outright. Black on green is 6.94:1. The kit therefore declares "text_on": "black" and "white_ok": "large_only", and the renderer has no reason to ever use the large-only path. The #00a890 value circulating in the house style is a typo from the guidelines' own accessibility table; the palette page gives #00a791.
