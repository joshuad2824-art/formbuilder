Why all four look the same. The rule the renderer enforces everywhere is that no meaning may be carried by color alone — up to about 8% of male readers have a color vision deficiency, and a photocopier has one hundred percent of the time. This kit takes that to its end: the four levels differ only in the word. Any document that still reads correctly here reads correctly on every kit, which makes this the right kit to draft on and the right one to test against.

What this kit does not have

No logo, no wordmark, no color field, no arch, no organization name. A document built on this kit carries its own title and nothing else. Drop a brand file in at any point and the same content re-renders with that brand — the content is data, so nothing is retyped.
