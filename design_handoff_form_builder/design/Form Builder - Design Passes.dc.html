What changed about signing PowerPlans

The 2018.01 build changes where a plan's orders land when you sign. The workflow looks identical and behaves differently, which is why this sheet exists.

What changed

Signing used to write every order to the profile at once. It now writes the phase you have open and holds the rest as Planned, so a plan signed at 07:00 no longer fires the discharge phase with the admission one.

Why it matters at the bedside

Nursing sees held orders in the profile but cannot act on them. If the phase is never initiated, they look complete to you and inert to them.

Important

A plan signed on the wrong encounter cannot be moved. It must be voided and re-ordered by the ordering provider.

What to do differently

Sign as you always have, then check the phase header before you leave the chart. If it reads Planned and you meant it to be active, right-click and choose Initiate Now. Ordering ahead for a later shift? Leaving it Planned is exactly right.

Three things this pass decided

11 pt, and the baseline unit is per-geometry. One 15 pt constant cannot divide three different live heights once leading is a function of measure. The invariant is that live height is an exact integer multiple of its own unit — 31 × 16.5, 37 × 15.75, 40 × 17.16.

The field guide bottom margin becomes 0.896 in. That is the only §7.1 number the 11 pt decision moves, and it is what buys the exact unit count.

Both letter sheets go to 0.75 in margins. The outer margin was never protecting the measure — but tightening it exposed the real constraint, which is the ceiling, not the floor. The two-column sheet moves its whitespace into a 0.875 in gutter to come back under 50 CPL; the prose variant becomes a 2/3 reading column plus a 1/3 rail, because a single column at 0.75 in runs 113 CPL.

Measure the string, never a constant. §11.2 says that for the engine; it applies to the spec's own numbers too. The 0.5 em fallback overstated every column by about a quarter and hid two out-of-band geometries. The field-guide table budget drops from 87 characters to 78 — a table authored to 87 would have overflowed its measure.
